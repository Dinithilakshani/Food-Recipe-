Ingredients

🛢️ 1 tablespoon canola oil
🧅 1 medium onion
🧄 1 tbsp crushed garlic
🥜 1 tbsp crushed ginger
🌶️ 1 tsp Araliya pepper powder
🥬 2 sliced leeks, washed
🍖 100g preferred meat substitute (e.g., shredded plant-based chicken)
🥕 3 grated carrots
🥚 4 eggs (or vegan egg substitute)
🧀 1 cup shredded vegan cheese
🧂 Salt and pepper to taste
🥟 6 chopped roti
🌶️ 1 tbsp Araliya un-roasted curry powder
🌶️ ¼ tsp Araliya pepper powder
🌶️ 2 tbsp Araliya chilli pieces

Directions

1

In a non-stick pan, heat canola oil over medium-high heat. Pan-fry the roti until golden brown.

2

In a wok, heat oil and scramble 4 eggs (or vegan substitute). Stir while frying.

3

Add canola oil to the wok, sauté onion until golden brown. Add 2 tsp Araliya chilli paste, ginger, garlic, leeks, and salt. Stir-fry for a few minutes.

4

Add meat substitute, Araliya pepper powder, chilli pieces, and curry powder. Stir-fry until vegetables are golden brown. Add carrots, mix, and season with salt.

5

For cheese sauce, melt butter (or vegan alternative), add flour and milk, whisking continuously. Add shredded vegan cheese, mix, and pour over koththu before serving.
