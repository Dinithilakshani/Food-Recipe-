Description

Follow these basic instructions for a thick, crisp, and chewy vegan pizza crust at home. The recipe yields enough pizza dough for two 12-inch pizzas, and you can freeze half of the dough for later. Close to 2 pounds of dough total.

Ingredients

💧 1 and 1/3 cups (320ml) warm water (between 100-110°F, 38-43°C)
🍞 2 and 1/4 teaspoons (7g) instant yeast (1 standard packet)
🍬 1 Tablespoon (13g) granulated sugar
🫒 2 Tablespoons (30ml) olive oil, plus more for pan and brushing
🧂 1 teaspoon salt
📏 3 and 1/2 cups (about 450g) all-purpose flour
🌽 Cornmeal, for dusting (optional)
🥫 Vegan pizza sauce, for topping
🧀 Vegan cheese, for topping
🌶️ Suggested toppings: bell peppers, mushrooms, or olives

Directions

1

Whisk warm water, yeast, and sugar in a stand mixer bowl with a dough hook or paddle attachment. Cover and rest for 5 minutes. If no mixer, use a large bowl and mix by hand in the next step.

2

Add olive oil, salt, and flour. Beat on low speed for 2 minutes or mix by hand.

3

Knead dough in the mixer or by hand on a lightly floured surface for 5 minutes. Add flour 1 teaspoon at a time if sticky, but keep dough soft. Test readiness with the windowpane test: stretch a small piece until thin; if light passes through without tearing, it’s ready.

4

Place dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise at room temperature for 60-90 minutes until doubled. For a warm environment, use a 150°F oven (turned off) with the door ajar.

5

Preheat oven to 475°F (246°C) for 15-20 minutes. Grease a baking sheet or pizza pan with olive oil; sprinkle with cornmeal for crunch.

6

Punch down dough, divide in half (freeze one half if desired). On a floured surface, flatten into a 12-inch disc, about 1/2-inch thick. Pinch edges to form a rim.

7

Cover dough and prepare toppings, such as vegan cheese, bell peppers, or mushrooms.

8

Push dents into dough to prevent bubbling, brush with olive oil, add toppings, and bake for 13-15 minutes until crust is golden.

9

Slice and serve immediately. Store leftovers in the refrigerator or freeze baked slices for up to 3 months.
